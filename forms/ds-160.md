# DS-160: fields and common mistakes

The DS-160 is the online application for a U.S. nonimmigrant visa, such as a visitor, student or work visa. Everyone who applies for one fills it in, one form per person, before the visa interview.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it is filled in | Online only, on the Consular Electronic Application Center (CEAC) website; there is no paper form to mail |
| Who fills it in | Every nonimmigrant visa applicant, including children and spouses, each with their own form |
| How long it takes | Most people need 60 to 90 minutes; the page times out after about 20 minutes without activity |
| Cost | No fee to submit the DS-160. The visa application fee (MRV fee) is a separate payment, and other charges, such as a visa integrity fee that some embassies collect when the visa is issued, may apply; check your embassy’s page for the amounts |
| What you bring | The confirmation page with its barcode, printed or saved, to the visa interview |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are not a U.S. citizen and want a nonimmigrant visa, such as a visitor, student, exchange or temporary work visa.
- You are a spouse or child who applies for a visa too: each person needs their own DS-160.
- You are the parent or guardian of a young child, or of someone who cannot do it themselves: you may complete and sign for them.

## Common mistakes

- **Choosing the wrong visa category.** The purpose and visa type you pick decide which visa you apply for. If it is wrong, you may need a new DS-160 and a new interview. Check which category fits before you choose.
- **A name that does not match the passport.** Copy your surname and given names exactly from the passport’s photo page, including middle names. Nicknames or a different spelling cause trouble at the interview.
- **Not saving the Application ID.** The portal times out after about 20 minutes without activity and unsaved answers are lost. Write the Application ID down at the start, press save, and use “Save Application to File” if you leave.
- **Waiting too long to finish.** An unfinished form is deleted if it sits untouched for about 30 days. Check the State Department’s DS-160 FAQ for the current rule, and finish it in one or two sittings.
- **Selecting the wrong embassy location.** The form is sent to the location you choose at the start and cannot be moved. Pick the post where you will have your interview, which generally has to be in your country of nationality or residence.
- **Using a photo that does not meet the rules.** The portal checks the photo. A selfie, glasses, a coloured background or a file that is too large or small can fail. Test the photo first, and keep a printed 2 x 2 inch photo in case the embassy asks for it.
- **Answering the Security and Background questions carelessly.** Answer every question truthfully and in full. A wrong answer, or one left out, such as an earlier visa refusal, can cause problems later.
- **Leaving fields blank or misusing Does Not Apply.** Use “Does Not Apply” and “Do Not Know” only when true. Do not use them to avoid a question you can answer.
- **Submitting without checking the review page.** You cannot edit a submitted form. Read every page of the Review step, and compare dates, passport number and the SEVIS ID with your documents.
- **Printing a confirmation page that is unclear.** The barcode must be readable. Also check that the barcode number on your appointment is the one from your latest DS-160.
