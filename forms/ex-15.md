# EX-15: fields and common mistakes

The EX-15 is the official form to apply for a Spanish foreigner identity number (NIE), or for a certificate of resident or non-resident. You fill it in if you are not Spanish and need the number for an economic, professional or social reason, such as buying property, opening a bank account or signing a contract.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | In Spain, a police station (Comisaría de Policía) or an immigration office (Oficina de Extranjería), usually by appointment; from abroad, the Spanish consulate for where you live |
| What to bring | The EX-15 signed, in original and 1 copy; your valid passport (EU citizens: national ID card) and a copy; the paid fee form (Modelo 790, código 012); and proof of your reason |
| Cost | The form is free; the fee is paid with Modelo 790, código 012. Check the amount on the official page |
| Decision time by law | 5 days from the day your application is registered; no answer in that time means it is refused |
| What you receive | A document with your NIE number. It is not a card and does not give you the right to live or work in Spain |

## Who fills it in

You fill in the EX-15 if:

- You are not Spanish and need an NIE to buy or sell property, open a bank account, sign a contract, accept an inheritance or represent a company.
- You are in Spain on a short stay or with a residence permit, or you live abroad and apply through a Spanish consulate.
- You already have an NIE and need a certificate of resident or non-resident.

## Common mistakes

- **Using the EX-15 when you need another form.** If you are an EU citizen moving to Spain to live, you register with the EX-18. If you have a long-stay visa, your NIE is already assigned; you apply for the TIE with the EX-17.
- **Giving no concrete reason.** The law requires you to say why you need the NIE. Tick the right box, write the reason under Especificar and bring proof, for example a property reservation contract or a letter from your bank.
- **Names that do not match your passport.** Write your names and surnames exactly as in your passport, in the same order and spelling. A difference can delay your application.
- **Paying the wrong fee.** On Modelo 790, código 012, choose the option for assigning an NIE, not the one for the TIE card. The two fees are different.
- **Bringing one unsigned copy.** Bring the form in original and 1 copy, signed, together with your original passport and a photocopy. An unsigned form is one of the most common mistakes.
- **Writing the date the wrong way round.** Write dates as day, month, year with 4 digits for the year: 03/07/1990, not 07/03/1990.
- **Ticking the electronic notification box without a certificate.** Tick the DEHú consent only if you have an electronic certificate or Cl@ve; without one, you cannot open the notification.
- **Booking the wrong appointment.** In Spain, book the police appointment for assigning an NIE in your province, not one for the TIE card, and bring the appointment confirmation.
