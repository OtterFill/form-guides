# EX-18: fields and common mistakes

The EX-18 is the official form to register in Spain’s Central Register of Foreigners as a resident EU, EEA or Swiss citizen; you receive a registration certificate that shows your NIE. You fill it in if you are an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen and will live in Spain for more than 3 months.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | In person, usually by appointment, at the immigration office (Oficina de Extranjería) of the province where you will live or, where there is none, the police station (Comisaría de Policía) |
| Deadline by law | Within 3 months of entering Spain |
| What to bring | The EX-18 signed, in original and 1 copy; your valid passport or national ID card; the paid fee form (Modelo 790, código 012); and proof of your situation: work, self-employment, studies, or resources and health insurance. Originals and copies |
| Cost | The form is free; the fee is paid with Modelo 790, código 012. Check the amount on the official page |
| What you receive | A registration certificate, issued straight away, with your name, nationality, address, NIE and date of registration |

## Who fills it in

You fill in the EX-18 if you are an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen and:

- You will live in Spain for more than 3 months as an employee, a self-employed worker or a student, or without working, with enough resources and health insurance.
- You are coming to live with, or are joining, a family member who is an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen and is in one of those situations.
- You have lived in Spain legally for 5 years without a break, or you are in one of the earlier cases for workers, and you want a certificate of permanent residence.
- You are already registered and need to update your data, for example a new address or passport, or cancel your registration.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting more than 3 months.** The law asks you to apply within 3 months of entering Spain. Appointments are limited in many provinces, so book one soon after you arrive.
- **Applying for an NIE instead.** If you are moving to Spain to live, the EX-15 is the wrong form: it gives you only a number. Register with the EX-18; the certificate shows your NIE.
- **No proof for the box you ticked.** Each option in section 4 needs its own proof: an employment certificate or contract for workers, enrolment for students, and resources plus health insurance if you do not work. Bring originals and copies.
- **Health insurance that does not cover Spain.** If you do not work, your insurance must cover you in Spain as fully as the public health system. Students may use a European Health Insurance Card only if it is valid for the whole stay.
- **Paying the wrong fee.** On Modelo 790, código 012, choose the option that includes the registration certificate of a resident EU citizen, not the one for assigning an NIE.
- **Forgetting the second signature.** If you ticked the family member option, the EU citizen your right comes from must also sign in the box for the citizen of the Union.
- **Not updating your registration.** You must tell the immigration office or police of a change of nationality, marital status or address. Use the EX-18 with MODIFICACIÓN.
- **Using the certificate as ID.** The certificate has no photo, so it does not prove who you are. Carry it with your passport or national ID card.
