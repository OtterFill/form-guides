# Cerfa 15763: fields and common mistakes

Cerfa 15763*02, known as the S1106, is the form you send to French public health insurance (Assurance Maladie) to open your right to have your healthcare costs covered when you are not yet attached to a French social security scheme. You fill it in when you start working in France or, if you do not work, once you live in France in a stable and regular way, usually after 3 months.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | The health insurance fund where you live: the CPAM (general scheme) or the MSA (farm scheme), by post or at the office |
| When | When you start working in France; without work, after 3 months of stable residence, unless you are in a situation the form exempts |
| What to attach | Your bank details (RIB with IBAN), a copy of your passport or ID card, a residence permit if you are not an EU/EEA or Swiss national, and proof of your work or of 3 months of residence |
| Cost | The application is free |
| Length | 2 pages: the form on the front, the list of documents on the back |

## Who fills it in

You fill in this form if you are not yet attached to a French social security scheme and:

- You work in France, whatever your nationality.
- You do not work and live in France in a stable and regular way, usually for more than 3 months.
- You are a family member (spouse, cohabiting partner, PACS partner) who joins or accompanies, to settle in France, an insured person who works or lives in France: the form says you fill it in too.
- You come back to live in France after living abroad.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying too early without work.** Without work you usually need more than 3 months of stable residence in France. Wait until you can prove it, unless the back of the form lists your situation as covered without delay.
- **Using this form when another country covers you.** Posted workers and pensioners covered by another country’s social security do not fill in this form. They give the fund an S1 form or an equivalent document.
- **Names in the wrong order.** Write your birth family name first, then any usage name, then all your first names in the order of your civil-status documents, spelled as in your passport.
- **Proof of residence that does not cover 3 months.** One bill is not enough. Send successive documents covering the last 3 months, such as rent receipts or energy bills, or a host’s statement with bills in their name.
- **An email address that is cut off.** On screen the email box takes only about 10 characters. Write the full address by hand on the printed form.
- **Forgetting the bank details or the residence permit.** The fund pays into your account, so attach a RIB with your IBAN. If you are not an EU/EEA or Swiss national, also attach a copy of a valid residence permit or document.
- **Adding your children on this form.** Minor children go on form S3705, which you send with yours.
- **Not reporting a change.** By signing you promise to tell the fund at once about changes to part A and about a move abroad. Keep your address up to date with the fund.
