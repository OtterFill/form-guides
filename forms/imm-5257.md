# IMM 5257: fields and common mistakes

IMM 5257, “Application for Visitor Visa (Temporary Resident Visa)”, is the form from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that asks who you are, what your passport says, why and for how long you want to visit Canada, and a set of background questions. Each person who applies for a visitor visa fills in one, and it is completed on a computer with Adobe Acrobat Reader.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | IRCC; you upload it in your IRCC secure account or send it on paper. The IRCC Portal has its own online questions, so you do not need this form there |
| Who fills it in | Each person applying for a visitor visa or transit visa; a parent or legal guardian signs for a person under 18 |
| How to open it | Download it to a computer and open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader version 10 or higher; it may not open on tablets or phones |
| Edition used here | IMM 5257 (09-2023) E |
| Cost | The form is free; the visa has a processing fee and most people also pay a biometrics fee. Check the official page for the current amounts |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are applying for a visitor visa or a transit visa in your IRCC secure account, or on paper.
- You are travelling with family: each family member files their own forms, although you may submit them together.
- You are helping a child: a parent or legal guardian completes and signs the form for a person under 18.

## Common mistakes

- **Changing the name from the passport.** Type the family name and given names exactly as in the passport, even if misspelled, and without initials. A different spelling can cause delays or a mismatch with other documents.
- **Writing NA in a name box.** If your passport has no family name, put your given names in the family name box and leave the given name box blank. Do not write “*”, “Not applicable” or “NA” in a name box.
- **Leaving background questions unanswered.** IRCC says every question in that section must be answered, or the application is considered incomplete and returned. Check No or Yes for each one, and give details when Yes.
- **Dates and details that do not match.** Visit dates, employment history, funds and addresses should agree with your tickets, letters and bank statements. Check each date in YYYY-MM-DD form before you validate.
- **Leaving gaps in the employment history.** The form asks for the past 10 years, with no gaps. If you were studying, retired or not working, say so.
- **Using the wrong funds currency.** The funds box is in Canadian dollars (CAD). Convert the amount first; do not type it in your own currency.
- **Forgetting the forms that go with it.** Depending on your case you may also need IMM 5645 (family information) from each adult, IMM 5409 for a common-law partner, a marriage certificate copy, IMM 5476 for a representative or IRM 0002 for a gender identifier change.
- **Opening it in a browser or on a phone.** IRCC says to use a computer and Adobe Acrobat Reader version 10 or higher. The form may not open on tablets or phones.
- **Sending the form when the Portal asks the questions instead.** If you apply in the IRCC Portal, you answer the questions online and do not upload IMM 5257. Check which route your application uses first.
