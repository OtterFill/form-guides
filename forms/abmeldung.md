# Abmeldung: fields and common mistakes

The Abmeldung is the form (Meldeschein) you hand in at the registration office (Meldebehörde) when you leave a flat in Germany without moving into another one in Germany, usually because you move abroad or give up a second home. You deregister from 1 week before until 2 weeks after you move out; if you only move within Germany, registering at the new address is enough.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | The registration office (Meldebehörde, often called Bürgeramt) of the town you move out of |
| Deadline | At the earliest 1 week before you move out, at the latest 2 weeks after (§ 17 of the Federal Registration Act, BMG) |
| How | In person or by post with a copy of your passport or ID card; some towns also online when you move abroad |
| Cost | Free of charge |
| Fine for deregistering late | Up to €1,000 (§ 54 BMG) |
| Proof you receive | A deregistration confirmation (Abmeldebestätigung) |

## Who fills it in

You deregister if:

- You move abroad and give up your flat in Germany, whatever your nationality.
- You give up one of several homes in Germany, for example a second home, without moving into a new one in Germany.
- You no longer have any fixed home in Germany.

## Common mistakes

- **Deregistering when you move within Germany.** You do not deregister for a move within Germany. Register at the new address (Anmeldung) within 2 weeks; that is enough.
- **Deregistering too early or too late.** The office accepts the Abmeldung at the earliest 1 week before you move out, and the deadline is 2 weeks after. Deregistering late can mean a fine of up to €1,000.
- **Posting the form without a copy of your ID.** When you deregister by post, add a copy of your passport or ID card, as the towns ask. Hamburg wants a copy for everyone aged 16 or older on the form.
- **Ticking Hauptwohnung for your only home.** If the flat was your only home in Germany, tick alleinige Wohnung. Hauptwohnung is for the main one of several homes.
- **Leaving out the new address abroad.** When you move abroad, write the new address and the country. The form asks for it, and the register keeps it.
- **Putting flatmates on your form.** Only spouses, partners, parents and children who leave the same flat for the same new address share one form. Anyone else fills in a form of their own.
- **Leaving Germany without the confirmation.** By post, processing can take several weeks (in Munich about 3 to 4). Deregister in time, because you need the confirmation to end contracts and the broadcasting fee.
- **Thinking the Abmeldung tells every authority.** It does not replace telling other bodies, for example the vehicle registration office, your health insurer or the broadcasting fee service (Rundfunkbeitrag).
