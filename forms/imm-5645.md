# IMM 5645: fields and common mistakes

IMM 5645, “Family Information”, is a form from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that lists your spouse or common-law partner, parents, children and brothers and sisters, with each person’s date and country of birth, marital status, address and occupation. Each person aged 18 or older applying for a visitor visa, or for a study or work permit from outside Canada, fills in their own.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | IRCC, with your visitor visa, study permit or work permit application, when your application package or document checklist lists it |
| Who fills it in | Each applicant aged 18 or older, one form each |
| What it covers | You, your spouse or common-law partner, your parents, all your children and all your brothers and sisters, on 2 pages |
| Edition used here | IMM 5645 (01-2021) E |
| Cost | The form is free. The application it goes with has fees: check the official page for the current amounts |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are 18 or older and applying for a Temporary Resident Visa (visitor visa), or for a study or work permit from outside Canada.
- Family members aged 18 or older are applying with you: each one fills in their own copy.
- Your application package or your document checklist names IMM 5645.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving out family who are not coming.** The form says to include ALL family members even if they are not accompanying you. List everyone and check NO in the accompany column for those who stay behind.
- **Missing some of your children.** Section B covers every child: married, adopted and step-children, children adopted by others, children living with an ex-spouse or another guardian, and children who are already Canadian citizens or permanent residents.
- **Leaving boxes blank.** IRCC says you must answer all questions. Where something does not apply, write “Not applicable” or “N/A”. The form warns that an incomplete form may delay your application or stop it being processed.
- **Forgetting Note 1 or Note 2.** If you have no spouse or common-law partner, sign and date Note 1 at the end of Section A. If you have no children, sign and date Note 2 at the end of Section B.
- **Writing names only in English.** The form asks for ALL names in English and in your native language, for example in Chinese, Arabic, Cyrillic, Korean or Japanese characters.
- **Details that differ from your other forms.** Names, dates of birth and marital status should match your main application, such as IMM 5257 or IMM 1294. The signed form becomes part of your Immigration Record and is used to check your family details on future applications.
- **Using the wrong family form.** IMM 5645 and IMM 5707 are both family information forms for visitors, students and workers. Fill in the one your application package or document checklist names.
- **Not signing, or printing to sign online.** On paper, sign and date Section D, or the form is sent back to you. If you apply online, IRCC says not to print the form to sign it; your electronic signature on the application counts.
- **Opening it in a browser or on a phone.** IRCC says to use a computer and Adobe Acrobat Reader version 10 or higher. The form may not open on tablets or mobile phones.
