# Anmeldung: fields and common mistakes

The Anmeldung is the form (Meldeschein) you hand in at your local registration office (Meldebehörde) to register the flat you have moved into. You must register within two weeks of moving in, with your passport or ID and a confirmation from your landlord.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | The registration office (Meldebehörde, often called Bürgeramt) of the town where the new flat is |
| Deadline | Within 2 weeks after you move in (§ 17 of the Federal Registration Act, BMG) |
| What to bring | The completed form, your passport or ID card, your residence permit if you have one, and the landlord’s confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung) |
| Cost | Free of charge |
| Fine for registering late | Up to €1,000 (§ 54 BMG) |
| Proof you receive | A confirmation of registration (Meldebestätigung); the tax ID is sent by post |

## Who fills it in

You register if:

- You have moved into a flat or house in Germany, as a tenant, owner or subtenant, whatever your nationality.
- You are a newcomer from abroad, or you moved within Germany to another town or another address.
- You live in a second flat besides your main home: it needs registering too.

## Common mistakes

- **Registering too late.** The deadline is two weeks after you move in, and a late registration can mean a fine of up to €1,000. Appointments can be scarce in big cities, so book early.
- **Coming without the landlord’s confirmation.** Without the Wohnungsgeberbestätigung the office cannot register you, and a lease does not replace it. Ask your landlord for it before you move in. If they refuse or are slow, tell the office without delay.
- **Ticking Hauptwohnung for your only home.** If it is your only home in Germany, tick alleinige Wohnung. Hauptwohnung is for the main one of several homes.
- **Names that do not match your documents.** Write every name as in your passport and civil-status documents, in the same order and spelling. A difference can cause problems with the tax ID and the bank.
- **Leaving out a former name or a citizenship.** Add a former name and all your citizenships. A missing entry is a common reason for the office to send you back.
- **A move-in date that differs from the landlord’s confirmation.** The date on the form and on the confirmation must match. Use the day you actually moved in.
- **Adding more than “Dr.”.** Only the abbreviation “Dr.” is entered, without “med.” or other additions. A foreign doctorate is entered only if you may use the title in Germany.
- **Thinking the Anmeldung tells every authority.** It does not replace telling other bodies about your move, for example the vehicle registration office.
