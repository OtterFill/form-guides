# Schengen visa: fields and common mistakes

The Schengen visa application form is the single EU form you complete to apply for a short-stay visa, meaning a stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Every traveller who needs a visa fills in and signs their own form and gives it to the consulate, or to the service provider the consulate uses, of the country they are visiting.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | The embassy or consulate of the competent Schengen country, or the service provider it uses |
| Who fills it in | Every traveller who needs a short-stay visa, each with their own form; a parent or legal guardian signs for a minor |
| When to apply | At least 15 calendar days before the trip and no more than 6 months before it starts |
| What it allows | A stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period; the consulate decides the number of entries and the validity |
| Cost | The form is free; the visa fee is a general EUR 90 for adults according to the Commission, with reductions and exemptions, so check the page of your consulate |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are a citizen of a country whose nationals need a visa for a short stay in the Schengen area: tourism, business, visiting family or friends, study, medical reasons or airport transit.
- You are applying for a minor: the form is filled in for the child, and the person with parental authority or the legal guardian signs.
- You are a family member of an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen: you fill in fields 17 and 18 and leave out the fields marked with an asterisk (*).

## Common mistakes

- **Applying to the wrong country.** Apply to the country where you will stay longest or where the main purpose of the trip happens; only if you cannot tell, to the country where you first enter. A consulate that is not competent returns the application and the fee.
- **Writing a name that differs from the passport.** Fields 1 to 3 must follow the travel document. Do not translate, shorten or reorder names, and do not leave out a second given name.
- **Leaving the form unsigned.** The form must be filled in and signed to be admissible. For a minor, the parent or guardian signs, and each person on a shared passport needs a separate form.
- **Applying too early or too late.** Lodge the application at least 15 calendar days before you travel and no more than 6 months before the trip starts. Check whether your consulate needs an appointment.
- **Using a passport that is not valid long enough.** It must be valid for at least 3 months after you plan to leave and have at least two blank pages. Renew it before you apply.
- **Forgetting field 27 or the dates.** Tick the number of entries you want and write the arrival and departure dates. The dates must fit your itinerary and the 90 days in any 180-day period.
- **Skipping the fingerprint answer in field 28.** Tell the consulate whether your fingerprints were taken for an earlier Schengen visa. If you leave it blank and do not remember, you may be asked to give them again.
- **Filling in the asterisk fields as an EU family member.** Family members of EU, EEA or Swiss citizens fill in fields 17 and 18 and leave out fields 21, 22, 30, 31 and 32.
- **Correcting a printed form by hand.** Some missions only accept the form completed online and printed with its barcode, and do not accept manual corrections. Fix errors in the online form and print it again.
- **Entering data that your documents do not support.** Your answers about purpose, host and costs must match your supporting documents. The form warns that false statements can lead to rejection, annulment of the visa and prosecution.
