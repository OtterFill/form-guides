# IMM 5708: fields and common mistakes

IMM 5708, “Application to Change Conditions, Extend my Stay or Remain in Canada as a Visitor or Temporary Resident Permit Holder”, is the form from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that people already in Canada use to stay longer as a visitor (a visitor record), restore their visitor status, or get a temporary resident permit. Each person applying fills in their own, and most upload it in their IRCC secure account.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | IRCC. You upload it in your IRCC secure account; paper is only for people who cannot apply online because of a disability or a problem with the online application |
| Who fills it in | Each person in Canada who wants to stay longer as a visitor, change to visitor status, restore visitor status, or get a temporary resident permit; every family member applies separately |
| When | Before your current status expires; IRCC advises at least 30 days before |
| Edition used here | IMM 5708 (09-2023) E |
| Cost | The form is free; extending your stay has a processing fee, and restoration and biometrics have their own fees. Check the official page for the current amounts |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are visiting Canada and want to stay longer than the date you were given at entry.
- You are a worker authorized to work without a work permit, or a student authorized to study without a study permit, and want to stay longer.
- Your study or work permit is ending and you want to stay on as a visitor.
- Your visitor status expired less than 90 days ago and you want to restore it.
- You are inadmissible to Canada and are applying for a first or new temporary resident permit.
- You are helping a child: each family member applies separately, and a parent or legal guardian signs for a person under 18.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying after your status expired.** If your status has already expired, you cannot extend it. Check the restore box instead, within 90 days. IRCC says a fee paid for the wrong application is not refunded.
- **Reading the wrong expiry date.** Your status ends on the date stamped in your passport at entry, not on the date of your visa. With no stamp, it ends 6 months after you entered; with a permit or visitor record, on the date printed on it.
- **A vague reason for staying.** IRCC asks for full details of why you want to stay longer and of your plans to leave Canada. Add a letter that explains both and matches the purpose and dates on the form.
- **Funds that do not match the proof.** The funds box is in Canadian dollars and should agree with your bank statements or a guarantor’s letter for the whole extra stay.
- **Asking for dates past your passport’s expiry.** Your status cannot outlast your passport. Renew it first, or ask only for dates within its validity.
- **Leaving background questions unanswered.** Every question in that section must be answered, or the application is returned as incomplete. A returned application counts as never submitted, so it does not keep your status.
- **Printing and signing the form for an online application.** Online, you upload the validated form without a signature and sign electronically at the end. A printed and scanned copy loses the barcode page.
- **Treating the visitor record as a visa.** A visitor record only lets you stay longer. If you leave Canada, you need a valid visitor visa or eTA to come back, unless you only visited the United States or St. Pierre and Miquelon and return within your authorized stay.
