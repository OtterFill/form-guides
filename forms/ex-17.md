# EX-17: fields and common mistakes

The EX-17 is the official form to apply for the Spanish foreigner identity card (TIE): the first card, a card after your permit is renewed, or a duplicate. You fill it in if you are not an EU citizen and Spain has given you a visa or a permit to stay for more than 6 months.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | In person, by appointment (cita previa), to the National Police documentation unit at the immigration office or police station of the province where you live. It cannot be filed online or at a public registry |
| Deadline by law | 1 month from your entry into Spain, or from the day your permit is granted or takes effect |
| What to bring | The EX-17 signed, in original and 1 copy; your passport; 1 recent colour photo on a white background, passport size; the paid fee form (Modelo 790, código 012); your visa if you have one; and the other documents your case needs |
| Cost | The form is free; the card fee is paid with Modelo 790, código 012 and depends on the type of card. Check the amount on the official page |
| What you receive | The TIE, a card with your photo and your NIE. It is valid as long as the permit it documents, and you collect it in person later |

## Who fills it in

You fill in the EX-17 if:

- You are not an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen and arrived in Spain with a visa for more than 6 months, for example to study, work or live without working.
- You were granted a permit to stay or live in Spain for more than 6 months while already in Spain.
- Your permit was renewed and you need the new card that goes with it.
- Your card was lost, stolen or damaged, or the data on it changed, and you need a duplicate.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying after the deadline.** You must apply within 1 month of entering Spain or of the day your permit is granted or takes effect. Book the appointment as soon as you can, because appointments are scarce in many provinces.
- **Using the EX-17 when you need another form.** EU, EEA and Swiss citizens register with the EX-18. Non-EU family members of EU citizens use the EX-19. The EX-17 is for everyone else with a visa or permit for more than 6 months.
- **Booking the wrong appointment.** Choose your province and the police option for fingerprints for the TIE (toma de huellas), not the one for an NIE or for an EU registration certificate. The exact wording varies by province.
- **Paying the wrong fee.** Modelo 790, código 012 has separate options for a first card, a renewal and a long-term residence card. Pick the one that matches your card, and use an original fee form: each one has its own number, so a photocopy is not accepted.
- **Ticking the wrong box in 4.1.** Tick TARJETA INICIAL for your first card, RENOVACIÓN DE TARJETA after your permit is renewed, and DUPLICADO only when the card is lost, stolen, damaged or its data changed.
- **Bringing one unsigned copy.** Bring the form in original and 1 copy, signed, together with your original documents and photocopies. The originals are checked and given back.
- **A photo that does not meet the rules.** Bring 1 recent colour photo, passport size, on a white background. Some universities’ guides ask for 32 × 26 mm with the whole face visible.
- **An address that does not match.** The card is issued in the province where you live. Write your real address in Spain, and bring a recent municipal registration certificate if you have moved.
