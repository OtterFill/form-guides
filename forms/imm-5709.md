# IMM 5709: fields and common mistakes

IMM 5709, “Application to Change Conditions, Extend my Stay or Remain in Canada as a Student”, is the form from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) that students already in Canada use to extend or change their study permit, get one for the first time, or restore their status as a student. Each applicant fills in their own: most download it from the document checklist in their IRCC secure account, validate it and upload it.

Official form: [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/imm5709.html) · Edition: IMM 5709 (06-2026) E · Last checked: 2026-10-26

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | IRCC. You upload it in your IRCC secure account; paper is only for people applying for a temporary resident permit with their permit, people who cannot apply online because of a disability, or when the online application has a problem |
| Who fills it in | Each person in Canada who extends or changes a study permit, applies for a first one from inside Canada, or restores their status as a student |
| When | Before your current status expires; IRCC advises at least 30 days before |
| Edition used here | IMM 5709 (06-2026) E. Since January 30, 2024, IRCC does not accept the old version in online applications |
| Cost | The form is free; the study permit has a processing fee, and restoration and biometrics have their own fees. Check the official page for the current amounts |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are in Canada with a study permit that is about to expire and you will keep studying.
- You are changing your school (DLI) or your level of study, or other conditions on your study permit.
- You are in Canada and eligible to apply for a first study permit from inside Canada, for example as the holder of a work or study permit, or their family member.
- Your student status expired less than 90 days ago and you want to restore it.
- You are helping a child: a parent or legal guardian signs the form for a person under 18.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying after the permit expires.** Apply before your status expires, and IRCC advises at least 30 days before. After it expires, an extension is no longer possible; you would have to restore your status within 90 days and pay a second fee.
- **Using an old copy of the form.** IRCC does not accept the version from before January 30, 2024 in online applications. Download the form from your document checklist each time you apply instead of reusing a saved copy.
- **Typing the DLI number with a zero.** The Designated Learning Institution number begins with the capital letter O, not the digit zero. Copy it exactly from your letter.
- **Funds that do not match the proof.** The funds box is in Canadian dollars and should agree with your bank statements or sponsor letters, and cover at least the tuition and costs you entered in question 4.
- **Answering No to “previously applied”.** Your first study permit, visas and other permits are earlier applications to Canada. Check Yes in question 2c) of the background section and list each one with its date.
- **Printing and signing the form for an online application.** Online, you upload the validated form without a signature and sign electronically at the end. A printed and scanned copy loses the barcode page.
- **A mailing address you will leave.** IRCC mails the new permit to the mailing address on the form. Use one where you will still receive mail, with your apartment or unit number, and update it in your account if you move.
- **Leaving Canada while you wait.** If you leave before the decision, you may come back only as a visitor and cannot study until the new permit is issued. Check the rules before you travel.
