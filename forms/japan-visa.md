# Japan visa: fields and common mistakes

The Visa Application Form to Enter Japan (査証申請書) is the 2-page form of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan that you complete to apply for a Japanese visa, for a short stay or, with a Certificate of Eligibility, for work, study or a long stay. Every applicant who needs a visa fills in and signs their own form and hands it in with a photo, a passport and supporting documents to the Japanese embassy or consulate for their place of residence, or to the agency it designates.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | The Japanese embassy or consulate with jurisdiction over where you live, or the accredited agency, visa centre or online system it designates |
| Who fills it in | Every applicant who needs a visa, each with their own form; a parent or legal representative signs for a minor |
| Processing time | 5 working days from the day after the application is accepted if there is no problem; longer if the embassy refers it to Tokyo |
| Validity | A single-entry visa must be used within 3 months; multiple-entry visas are valid for 1 to 5 years |
| Cost | The form is free; from 1 July 2026 the fee is about 15,000 yen for a single entry and 30,000 yen for multiple entries, it varies by nationality, and nothing is charged if the visa is not issued |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- Your nationality needs a visa for a short stay in Japan: up to 90 days for tourism, business, visiting relatives or friends and similar purposes, without paid work.
- You are going to work, study or live in Japan for longer than 90 days: in principle someone in Japan first gets you a Certificate of Eligibility, and you apply for the visa with it.
- You are applying for a child: the form is filled in for the child, and a parent or legal representative signs it.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving fields blank.** Embassy instructions ask you to complete every field and write “N/A” or “None” where something does not apply. Only the two lines marked (Note) are optional.
- **Writing a name that differs from the passport.** Copy the surname and given names exactly from the data page. The details on your documents must match your passport.
- **Signing digitally or not at all.** Print the form and sign it by hand in ink. For a child, a parent or legal representative signs and writes the relationship.
- **Stapling or reusing an old photo.** Glue a 45mm × 35mm photo taken within the last 6 months; do not staple or tape it.
- **Ticking No for a suspended sentence.** Note 2 says to tick Yes even if the sentence was suspended. Explain any Yes answer in the details box.
- **Writing dates in the wrong order.** Every date on the form is day, month, year. A date in another order can make your trip or passport look wrong.
- **Applying at the wrong embassy.** Apply to the Japanese embassy or consulate with jurisdiction over where you live, not where you happen to be travelling.
- **Answers that do not match the itinerary.** Your length of stay, arrival date, port of entry, flight and hotel must match your itinerary and other documents.
- **Reapplying too soon after a refusal.** MOFA does not accept a new application for the same purpose within 6 months of a refusal, unless your circumstances changed significantly; ask the embassy first.
