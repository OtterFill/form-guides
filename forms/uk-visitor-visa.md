# UK visitor visa: fields and common mistakes

The UK Standard Visitor visa application is the online GOV.UK form for people who need a visa to visit the UK for tourism, family, business or a short course. Each traveller fills in their own application before travelling, then gives fingerprints and a photo at a visa application centre.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it is filled in | Online only, on GOV.UK; there is no paper form. After paying, you book an appointment at a visa application centre |
| Who fills it in | Anyone whose nationality needs a visa to visit the UK, one application per person, children included |
| When to apply | The earliest is 3 months before you travel. A decision usually comes within 3 weeks of your appointment; check the official page for current times |
| Cost | £135 for a visa of up to 6 months when this guide was checked; long-term visas cost more. Fees change, so check the official page |
| How long you can stay | Usually up to 6 months per visit |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You need a visa to visit the UK for a holiday, to see family or friends, for business meetings, a short course of up to 6 months or another permitted activity.
- You are travelling with family: each person needs their own application, fee and appointment.
- You are applying for your partner or child who cannot apply themselves: you may fill it in for them.

## Common mistakes

- **Not declaring an earlier refusal.** A visa refusal or refusal of entry in any country must be declared. Hiding one can be treated as deception, leading to refusal and a ban of up to 10 years. Declare it and explain it.
- **Money answers that do not match the bank statements.** Income, savings and costs on the form should match your documents. Explain any large deposit and where it came from; statements more than 1 year old carry little weight.
- **A purpose that does not match the documents.** The reason and activities you give must match your letters, invitation and plans. A vague or changing purpose raises doubts.
- **A name that differs from the passport.** Write your names exactly as in the passport you will travel with, and list any other names you have used officially.
- **Skipping or guessing the travel history.** Go through your passports and give every trip asked for in the last 10 years. If you are not sure of the number of UK visits, give an estimate rather than leaving it out.
- **Ticking “no parents’ details” for convenience.** That option is only for people who do not know a parent. If you just do not have the details with you, ask your parents first.
- **Buying tickets that cannot be refunded.** UKVI advises not to buy travel tickets until the visa is issued, and hotel and flight bookings are weak evidence. If you book, choose something you can cancel for free.
- **Accepting the declaration before checking.** After the declaration you cannot change your answers in the form. Use Check your answers and compare everything with your documents first.
- **Sending documents without translations.** Every document not in English or Welsh needs a full certified translation, or it may not be considered.
