# W-7: fields and common mistakes

Form W-7 is the IRS application for an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN), the 9-digit U.S. tax number for people who need one but cannot get a Social Security number, and for renewing an ITIN that has expired. Each person applying, including a spouse or child, fills in their own form, usually attached to the U.S. federal tax return that needs the number.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Who receives it | The IRS ITIN Operation in Austin, Texas, by mail, or in person at an IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center or through an acceptance agent |
| What goes with it | Your U.S. federal tax return, unless an exception applies, and original identity documents or copies certified by the issuing agency |
| Cost | Free from the IRS; acceptance agents may charge a fee |
| Processing time | About 7 weeks; 9 to 11 weeks from January 15 to April 30 or if you apply from abroad |
| How long it lasts | An ITIN not used on a federal tax return for 3 years in a row expires on December 31 of the third year |

## Who fills it in

You fill it in if:

- You are not a U.S. citizen or green card holder.
- You do not have a Social Security number and are not eligible for one.
- You need a U.S. tax number for a federal tax purpose: to file a U.S. tax return, to be claimed on someone else's return for an allowable tax benefit, or for one of the exceptions in the instructions.
- Or you already have an ITIN that has expired and you need it on a tax return: you use the same form to renew it.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending the form without the tax return.** Unless an exception applies, attach Form W-7 to the front of your tax return and mail them together to the ITIN address, not to the address in the tax return's own instructions.
- **Ordinary photocopies of your documents.** Send originals or copies certified by the agency that issued them. A copy certified by a notary is not enough, except for some U.S. military families.
- **Expired documents.** Your passport or ID must still be valid on the day you submit the application.
- **Writing “ITIN renewal” as the reason.** Renewal is the “Renew an existing ITIN” box at the top. You must still tick one reason box, a to h.
- **Blank sections.** Write N/A in each part of a line that does not apply. Line 4, for example, has 3 separate entries.
- **Details that do not match your documents.** Your name, date of birth and citizenship on lines 1a, 1b, 4 and 6a must be the same as in your documents. Write dates month first.
- **A child's passport without a U.S. entry date.** For most dependents, such a passport is not enough on its own: add proof of U.S. residency, such as a U.S. school or medical record, as the instructions list by age.
- **Filing the return electronically.** A return with Form W-7 goes on paper, and you cannot e-file a return with your ITIN in the calendar year it is assigned.
