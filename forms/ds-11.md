# DS-11: fields and common mistakes

Form DS-11 is the U.S. State Department’s application for a passport book or card made in person. You use it for your first passport, for a child under 16, and whenever you cannot renew by mail with Form DS-82.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Where it goes | In person only: to an acceptance facility (many post offices, libraries and local government offices), a passport agency by appointment, or a U.S. embassy or consulate abroad. It cannot be mailed in or done online |
| Who uses it | First-time applicants, children under 16, and anyone whose last passport was issued under 16, more than 15 years ago, or was lost, stolen or damaged |
| Signing | Do not sign at home. You sign in front of the acceptance agent, after they administer an oath |
| What you bring | The unsigned form, proof of U.S. citizenship and a photo ID, each with a photocopy of the front and back, one 2 x 2 inch colour photo, and the fees |
| Cost | Two payments: the passport fee to the U.S. Department of State and a separate execution fee to the facility. Fees are not refundable; check travel.state.gov for the current amounts and processing times |

## Who fills it in

You must use the DS-11 if at least one of these is true:

- You are applying for your first U.S. passport.
- You are under 16, or you are applying for a child under 16.
- Your most recent passport was issued when you were under 16.
- Your most recent passport was issued more than 15 years ago.
- Your most recent passport was lost, stolen or damaged, or you cannot send it in.
- Your name has changed and you cannot document the change in a way that lets you use Form DS-82 or DS-5504.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing the form at home.** Sign only when the acceptance agent asks you to, after the oath. Bring the form to the appointment unsigned.
- **Using the wrong form.** If your passport was issued more than 15 years ago, when you were under 16, or was lost, stolen or damaged, you cannot renew by mail with the DS-82. Use the DS-11 and apply in person. If you meet every DS-82 condition, you can renew by mail instead.
- **Correcting a mistake on the form.** Do not cross out, write over or use white-out. If you make an error, fill in a new form. The Form Filler avoids most handwriting problems; print its result on one side of the paper only.
- **Leaving boxes empty.** Missing answers delay the application, and the sex box is often forgotten. Answer every item that applies to you, and write “none” for travel plans if you have none.
- **Bringing the wrong photocopies.** You need a photocopy of the front and back of your citizenship document and of your ID, on plain white 8.5 x 11 inch paper, one side only. Photocopies are not returned.
- **Bringing only a digital ID.** A mobile driver’s licence or other digital ID on your phone is not accepted. Bring a physical photo ID; with a temporary licence or learner’s permit you may be asked for an extra ID. Also bring the original or certified citizenship document, not just a copy.
- **A photo that does not meet the rules.** Glasses, a photo older than 6 months, a coloured background or the wrong size are common reasons for delay. Have a 2 x 2 inch passport photo taken; some acceptance facilities, such as many post offices, take them.
- **Coming for a child without both parents.** For a child under 16, both parents or guardians should come with the child. If one cannot, bring their notarized DS-3053, no more than 3 months old, with a copy of their ID, or proof that you have sole custody.
- **Paying everything in one payment.** The passport fee and the execution fee are paid separately, often to different payees. Check the accepted payment methods with the facility before you go.
