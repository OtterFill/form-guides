# Form guides

Plain-language notes on 17 popular tax, immigration and registration forms: what each form is, who fills it in, every field as printed on the form, and the mistakes people make. Each form links to a full field-by-field guide in 24 languages on [otterfill.com](https://otterfill.com/en/guides?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=form-guides).

Every guide is written from the issuer's own form and instructions and is checked against the official form; the date is on each page.

## Languages

The full guides are in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Русский, Українська, Türkçe, العربية, فارسی, اردو, हिन्दी, বাংলা, 日本語, 한국어, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ไทย, Filipino. Replace `/en/` in a guide's address with the language code (`en`, `zh`, `zh-Hant`, `es`, `fr`, `de`, `pt`, `it`, `nl`, `pl`, `ru`, `uk`, `tr`, `ar`, `fa`, `ur`, `hi`, `bn`, `ja`, `ko`, `id`, `vi`, `th`, `tl`).

## Data

`data/index.json` lists every form. `data/<slug>.json` has the form's facts (issuer, edition, official page, the day it was last checked), its fields grouped as on the form (number and label exactly as printed), the terms it uses, and the guide's address in every language.

## Fill a form

[OtterFill](https://otterfill.com/en?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=form-guides) reads any form (PDF, scan, Word or Excel), explains every question in your language, prefills answers from your documents and exports the filled original. Free to try.
